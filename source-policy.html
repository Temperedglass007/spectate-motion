How we report

Facts automatically.
Analysis deliberately.
Speculation transparently.

SPECTATE is an independent motorsport publication. We separate established facts from our interpretation and from stories that remain unconfirmed.

Updated 30 Aug 2026Newsroom standardVersion 1.1

01 / Primary sources first.

For Formula 1 news, SPECTATE prioritises Formula1.com, FIA documents and official team or driver statements. Official race classifications, schedules and regulations are treated as the record for those facts.

02 / A reputable report is not automatically a confirmed fact.

When a publication reports a claim, we distinguish the report from the underlying fact. If the underlying event has not been established by a primary source or sufficient evidence, SPECTATE does not label it Confirmed simply because the outlet is reputable.

03 / Three editorial labels.

CONFIRMED

An established fact supported by primary evidence or sufficiently established reporting. The label does not mean every interpretation around the fact is confirmed.

ANALYSIS

Original SPECTATE interpretation, context, prediction or editorial judgement. Analysis is clearly separated from reporting.

SPECULATION

A rumour, possible future move or unconfirmed claim. We state what is known, what is reported and what remains unproven.

04 / We do not copy.

SPECTATE uses source material to establish facts and then writes its own reporting and analysis. We link to the original publisher when readers need the underlying report.

05 / Images must match the story.

News images should be directly relevant to the subject: the driver, team, car, circuit, event or document being discussed. We do not use a generic F1 image as a substitute for a story-specific visual when a suitable image is available. Images are credited and licensed where required.

06 / Quotes are treated carefully.

Driver, team and official quotes must be attributable to a source. SPECTATE does not invent, stitch together or loosely paraphrase quotation marks. When a quote is not necessary, we paraphrase instead.

07 / Corrections.

If we find that a fact, attribution, image credit or label is wrong, we correct it rather than silently allowing the error to remain. The newsroom standard is accuracy over speed.

Our rule

If we cannot establish it, we label it.

That is the line between a SPECTATE report and a rumour presented as fact.
