Early winner call

Editorial probabilities · 15 Sep

Kimi Antonelli

27%

Championship leader arrives with eight wins from 14 races and a large points cushion. The case is strong, but Baku’s long straights and street-circuit volatility add uncertainty.

Max Verstappen

23%

Verstappen finished second at Madring and Baku rewards straight-line efficiency, braking and qualifying position.

Lando Norris

21%

McLaren lost the Spanish GP after the VSC/pit sequence, but Norris had the pace to win and remains a major threat.

Charles Leclerc

14%

Ferrari has shown race-winning pace this season and Leclerc remains a strong street-circuit candidate.

George Russell

9%

Russell is second in the championship but has publicly shifted his focus away from the title after falling 81 points behind Antonelli.
