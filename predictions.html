Round 13 / Italian GP

The model met
the race.

SPECTATE's pre-race desk had Russell 25%, Gasly 23%, Leclerc 18%, Piastri 14%, Hamilton 10% and Antonelli 6%. The actual winner was Antonelli — from P19.

Prediction review

Editorial probabilities vs confirmed outcome

Kimi Antonelli

6% → P1

The pre-race desk correctly recognised Antonelli's pace but heavily discounted the difficulty of a P19 start. The VSC/tyre sequence made the low-probability path real.

George Russell

25% → P2

Russell was the strongest conventional pick and led for much of the race, but tyre life prevented the win.

Pierre Gasly

23% → P7

Pole was converted into early track position, but Gasly could not sustain the lead through the strategic phases.

Charles Leclerc

18% → DNF

The home-race starting position became irrelevant after the lap-two crash.

Verdict

Low-probability paths matter.

The biggest lesson is not that a 6% estimate was “wrong”. It is that the model underweighted the combination of Antonelli's raw pace, Monza overtaking, the early red flag and a well-timed VSC pit stop.

No live telemetry was used in the original probabilities.

Next model

Madrid changes the variables.

The next forecast will be built from the completed Monza result, current championship standings and the new Madring's first F1 weekend — not from the old Monza snapshot.
