Singapore / Sprint review

The wet Sprint
changed everything.

A delayed start, heavy rain and nine retirements turned the Singapore Sprint into survival racing. Verstappen won, Hamilton and Leclerc gave Ferrari a double podium, and the McLaren pair collided on the final lap. These are Sprint results, not the Grand Prix finishing order.

What decided the Singapore Sprint?

Sprint review · 10 Oct

Wet-weather chaos

HIGH

Heavy rain delayed the start by more than 30 minutes. Nine cars retired, a record for an F1 Sprint, and incidents reshaped the order.

Verstappen holds on

P1

George Russell passed Verstappen on lap one, then crashed at the final turn; Verstappen retook the lead and won.

Antonelli salvages P4

P2

Championship leader Kimi Antonelli finished fourth after the late McLaren collision reshaped the final lap.

McLaren collision

DNF

Both McLaren drivers collided on the final lap, opening the door for Antonelli to move into fourth.
