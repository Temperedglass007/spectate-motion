Championship pulse · editorial assessment

Antonelli has the buffer.

A 102-point lead gives Antonelli a strong championship position, but Austin’s sprint-free Grand Prix weekend still offers 25 points to the race winner. The exact clinching scenarios depend on the final points and tie-break rules.

Verstappen’s consecutive wins make him a momentum threat for race honours, but that alone does not make him a title favourite. These are qualitative editorial assessments, not statistical forecasts.
