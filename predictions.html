The probability desk / Round 13

Who wins
at Monza?

The Dutch GP is now filed: Lando Norris won from pole, with Kimi Antonelli second and George Russell third. This Monza snapshot shifts the win view toward the drivers and teams best suited to a low-drag, high-speed circuit.

Italian GP win view

Editorial probability snapshot before Monza qualifying. These are estimates, not bookmaker odds or a live model.

Charles Leclerc

24%

Ferrari's home race, Monza's long straights and Leclerc's qualifying ceiling make him the leading single-driver pick in this snapshot.

Lando Norris

22%

Back-to-back wins in Hungary and the Dutch GP strengthen the form signal. McLaren's race pace keeps Norris near the top, though Monza's characteristics are different from Zandvoort.

Kimi Antonelli

20%

Antonelli arrives as championship leader after finishing second at Zandvoort. Mercedes' overall 2026 strength keeps him firmly in the win conversation.

George Russell

14%

Russell's Dutch GP Sprint win and third-place Grand Prix finish are strong recent signals, but the desk gives him less weight than the top three for Monza.

Oscar Piastri

8%

A McLaren win remains plausible, but the post-Dutch result and current evidence leave Piastri below Norris in this snapshot.

Max Verstappen

5%

Red Bull remains dangerous, but Verstappen's opening-lap retirement at Zandvoort means the current snapshot applies a conservative pre-Monza confidence level.

Rest of field

7%

Ferrari's second car, Hamilton and the rest of the field retain an outside path through qualifying, strategy, weather and reliability.

What changed

Dutch GP result reset the desk.

Norris is now on a two-race winning run, while Antonelli extended his championship lead with second at Zandvoort. Monza is a new test: long straights, low drag and heavy braking place a different premium on the car's efficiency.
