# SPECTATE MOTION - Launch source policy

## Live pages included in this build

- `calendar.html`: 2026 race calendar, sourced from Formula1.com's official 2026 calendar on 14 August 2026.
- `guide-2026.html`: 2026 regulations guide, sourced from official Formula 1 and FIA explainers on 14 August 2026.

## Source hierarchy for future automated summaries

1. FIA / Formula1.com official statements, regulations, timing and calendars
2. Team press releases and verified official team channels
3. Named reporting from established motorsport publications
4. Clearly-labelled speculation only, never presented as confirmation

## Labels

- **CONFIRMED** - official source or direct on-record confirmation
- **REPORTED** - named credible publication, awaiting official confirmation
- **SPECULATION** - discussion or prediction; not treated as fact
- **ANALYSIS** - SPECTATE MOTION viewpoint, assumptions stated

## Publishing rule

Every automatic item should display its source link, source-published date, SPECTATE MOTION update time, and label. Do not reproduce broadcast footage, paywalled copy, or full third-party articles; publish original summaries and link back to source material.
