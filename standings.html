The SPECTATE read

The leader has a gap. The grid still has a story.

Antonelli's 50-point advantage over Hamilton is substantial, but the visual spread matters: Mercedes owns positions one and three, Ferrari holds two and four, while Norris sits fifth after winning in Hungary. The Dutch Sprint has now added another layer to the weekend, with George Russell winning ahead of Charles Leclerc and Lando Norris.
