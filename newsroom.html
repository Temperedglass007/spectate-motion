Unconfirmed claims stay here until the underlying fact is established.

Speculation · explicitly unconfirmed

Reported personnel changes remain outside the confirmed desk.

Stories based on unnamed sources, paddock talk or possible future moves are not promoted to confirmed status simply because a reputable outlet reported them. They remain speculation until an official statement or other primary evidence establishes the underlying fact.

Policy · No breaking-news language used

Our newsroom rule

Facts automatically. Analysis deliberately. Speculation transparently. The green label means the underlying fact is established — not merely that somebody reputable reported it.
