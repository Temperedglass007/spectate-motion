F1, redesigned

Lighter cars, active aerodynamics, a different energy story and the end of traditional DRS: the essential changes shaping this new era.

01 / Power

More electric influence.

The 2026 power unit changes the balance of performance: roughly half comes from internal combustion and half from electrical power. The MGU-H is removed, while MGU-K output rises to 350 kW. The cars are also set to use 100% advanced sustainable fuel.

Recharge

Energy can be recovered through braking, part throttle, lift-and-coast and other mapped strategies. Drivers and teams must manage that battery state through a lap.

Boost

A driver-operated deployment tool that can apply saved electrical energy through a maximum or team-configured power setting to attack or defend.

02 / Aero

Two wing states,
not one DRS flap.

Active aerodynamics adjust both front and rear wing angles. In Corner Mode, the wings are configured for downforce; in Straight Mode, the elements open to reduce drag. Unlike old DRS, the aero system is not inherently dependent on being within one second of a rival.

Overtake Mode

This is the new proximity-based aid. If a driver is within one second at the detection point, the system makes extra energy available for a stronger electrical profile on the next lap.

Important distinction

Active Aero refers to the wing-state system. Overtake Mode is the proximity-triggered energy advantage. They are related to racing, but they are not the same tool.

03 / The car

Smaller. Lighter.
Less drag.

Official explainers outline a shorter 3.4m maximum wheelbase, a 1.9m floor-width target, narrower tyres and a lower minimum weight. The ground-effect floor tunnels used by the prior generation are removed in favour of a flatter floor concept, while the cars are designed to cut drag substantially.

Weight

Formula 1's official regulation explainers describe a meaningful reduction from the prior-generation 800kg baseline. The precise published figure varies across staged explainers and regulation updates; the governing text is definitive.

Safety

Updates include stronger roll-hoop crash-test requirements and a two-stage nose-cone approach intended to add protection in secondary impacts.

04 / The grid

An 11th team
changes the maths.

Cadillac's arrival expands the grid to 22 cars. Under the adjusted qualifying format, six drivers are eliminated in Q1 and Q2, while Q3 remains a 10-car contest.
