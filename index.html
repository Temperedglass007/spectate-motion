Sprint report / Singapore

Verstappen wins the chaos.
Ferrari takes P2 and P3.

Max Verstappen won a chaotic, rain-delayed Singapore Sprint. Lewis Hamilton finished second and Charles Leclerc third, while nine cars retired — a record for an F1 Sprint. George Russell crashed out late; the McLaren pair collided on the final lap.
