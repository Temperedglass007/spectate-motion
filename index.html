Championship

The Drivers’ title is decided; Mercedes now targets the Constructors’ crown.

Antonelli has clinched the Drivers’ title after Mexico City. Antonelli sealed the Drivers’ Championship at the Mexico City Grand Prix. Mercedes can mathematically clinch the Constructors’ title in São Paulo.

Red Bull

Verstappen wins in Mexico City.

Verstappen won the Mexico City Grand Prix from Lewis Hamilton and Kimi Antonelli. Hamilton finished 0.799 seconds behind, with Antonelli 4.006 seconds back; third place secured Antonelli’s title.
