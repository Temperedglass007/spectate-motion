Norris masters the rain.
Mercedes take the crown.

Drivers’ Championship

390 points · World Champion.

After São Paulo, Kimi Antonelli leads the Drivers’ Championship on 390 points, 111 clear of Max Verstappen (279). George Russell is third on 262, Lewis Hamilton fourth on 261, Charles Leclerc fifth on 251 and Lando Norris sixth on 246.

Championship

Mercedes complete the 2026 title double.

Kimi Antonelli secured the Drivers’ title in Mexico City. George Russell and Antonelli finished fourth and fifth at Interlagos, sealing Mercedes’ ninth Constructors’ Championship.

Red Bull

Norris wins at Interlagos.

Norris won the 71-lap São Paulo Grand Prix by 14.602 seconds from Verstappen, who recovered from 21st on the grid. Isack Hadjar completed the podium.

Next race

Las Vegas is Round 21.

The Las Vegas Grand Prix runs 19–21 November. Qualifying is Saturday 21 November at 09:30 IST and the 50-lap race begins Sunday 22 November at 09:30 IST.
