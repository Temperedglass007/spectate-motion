SPANISH GP / MADRID PREVIEW

Formula 1 arrives at the brand-new 5.414km Madring for Round 14. FP1 is the first official F1 running at the circuit; qualifying is Saturday and the race is Sunday.

CONFIRMED / MONZA RACE

Antonelli won from 19th, becoming the first Italian to win the Italian Grand Prix since 1966. The race featured an early red flag after Charles Leclerc's crash and a decisive mid-race VSC strategy call.

Strategy post-mortem

What actually decided Monza

Mercedes

VSC gamble paid.

Antonelli pitted under the mid-race Virtual Safety Car for fresh mediums while Russell stayed out on used hards. The tyre delta became decisive late.

Russell

Track position, then fade.

Russell led for much of the race but lacked the fresh rubber needed to answer Antonelli's late attack.

Red Bull

Verstappen P3.

Max briefly entered the lead fight and finished third, 10.861s behind Russell.

Race incidents

Red flag. Gravel. Recovery.

Lap 2: Leclerc crashed at the Parabolica and the race was red-flagged after the barrier damage. Restart: Antonelli was already into the upper midfield and continued his charge. Lap 29: Lance Stroll stopped, triggering the VSC that created Antonelli's winning tyre strategy. Late race: Antonelli briefly ran into the gravel while attacking Russell, then recovered and retook the lead.

Other incidents / steward notes

The rest of the Monza incident log.

RESTART

Tsunoda grid-position confusion

The first standing restart was aborted after Yuki Tsunoda was out of position in his grid box. Audi later lodged an intention to appeal after Tsunoda escaped a penalty for the starting procedure.
