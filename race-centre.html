Pierre Gasly shocked the field to take his first Formula 1 pole position for Alpine at Monza. His 1:21.786 beat George Russell by 0.060s.

Gasly is the official pole winner. Russell qualified second, Piastri third, Leclerc fourth and Hamilton fifth. Kimi Antonelli qualified seventh but will start from the back because of his engine-change penalty.
